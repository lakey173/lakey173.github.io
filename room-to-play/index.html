04 / Addressing the tradeoffs

What needs to be solved?

Support for an idea and scrutiny of its implementation can coexist. These are the concerns respondents explicitly selected.

Top concerns

680 answered · 54 missing · multiple selections allowed

The “No concerns” option was counted whenever selected, including on forms that also selected one or more specific concerns.

A constructive checklist

Design and governance questions for the next phase — suggested from feedback, not promises of the project

Traffic and parking

Publish transportation, access, tournament-day parking and neighborhood impact assessments.

Preserving recreation options

Explain impacts on public golf and evaluate options to mitigate loss of affordable access.

Affordable youth participation

Define local team access, equitable field allocation, fees and programming for families.

Funding and oversight

Share capital costs, operating assumptions, governance structure and decision milestones.

Environmental and neighborhood impacts

Address lighting, noise, trees, drainage and public open space before selecting a final design.

An important distinction: the survey measured public concerns; it did not establish engineering feasibility, total costs, the project’s economic return, or a finalized development plan.
